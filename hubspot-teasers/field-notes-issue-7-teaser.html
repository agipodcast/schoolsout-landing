School's Out Field Notes · Issue 7

The Age of Intelligence

For more than two years, I have interviewed educators, ed tech people, and administrators about AI implementation. Hundreds of hours of conversation keep sorting into four buckets: safety and security, teacher tool use, student tool use, and government policy.

The curriculum is missing from all four.

This Field Note opens a fifth bucket. It asks what every room in the school has already inherited now that AI is becoming a condition of the world students are entering.

AI is closer to electricity than a lamp. The wiring changes every room. Read the issue, then ask one department two questions: What do we keep? What have we already inherited?
