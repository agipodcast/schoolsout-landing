The Staff-Room AI Test

Last weekend, I put three AI systems into the same workstream. They were fast, capable, and perfectly willing to repeat each other's work until I gave them separate jobs.

Teachers know the pattern. “Everybody help” can leave four people editing the same slide and nobody calling the parent.

This Field Note offers a 15-minute staff-room test: give AI one job, one boundary, and one receipt. Then compare notes with another teacher. You will learn more from what the tool gets wrong than you will from another giant list of approved tools.
