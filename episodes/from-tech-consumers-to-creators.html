From Tech Consumers to Creators: AI, Agency, and Student Work with Alexander Kutulas

Watch on YouTube Listen on Spotify Listen on Apple Podcasts

About this conversation

Students are entering a job market where AI can produce code, applications, and polished personal brands faster than ever. So what actually helps a young person stand out?

Alexander Kutulas and Jason Padgett dig into agency, real-world experience, student creativity, and the skills that matter when the technical floor keeps rising. Alexander brings a student-builder's view from Purdue, where he studies computer science, organizes creative technical communities, and works on better ways to translate club experience into career evidence.

The conversation moves from hackathons and cross-campus collaboration to entry-level hiring, fractional work, AI literacy, and the difference between consuming technology and creating with it.

Why clubs, projects, and real experiences can teach what coursework alone cannot

How AI changes the value of technical skill without removing the need for judgment

Why students should learn to create with technology instead of only consuming it

What employers miss when they rely on resumes and polished online brands

Why agency, adaptability, and human conversation matter in an uncertain future

Try this after the episode: choose one experience outside the classroom and document what you built, who you worked with, what changed, and what you learned.
