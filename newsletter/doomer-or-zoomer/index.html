Doomer Or Zoomer?

Seven hypothetical AI decisions. Answer honestly, the meter doesn't judge, and neither do I. Mostly.

A playful self-check, not a validated assessment, prediction or measure of AI expertise. The scenarios are invented.

By Jason Padgett, your No-Code Chief AI Officer

DoomerZoomer
0 of 7 answered

Here's my take, and I've road-tested it every Friday morning over coffee with engineers, business owners, and at least one guy who quotes the Matrix like scripture: the useful position is neither doomer nor zoomer. It's the person who asks "okay, but what do I actually do on Tuesday?" The extinction debate and the utopia debate are both spectator sports. Your business, your classroom, your Sunday night, those are the field.

From The Quiz To Your Week

These scenarios are fictional. Try them against one decision you actually face: which model to pay for, how to check a vendor claim, or whether an automation still does its job. Your answers are a starting point for a conversation, not a scorecard for getting AI right.

What It Means For You

The loudest voices want you paralyzed or euphoric, because both keep you watching. Meanwhile the practical questions sit unanswered: does your county have backups for its critical systems, does your vendor demo survive contact with a real Tuesday, does the model you're paying for match the work you're actually doing. Boring questions. Boring questions are where the money is.

One Thing To Try

This week, take one task you do every Sunday night, cash flow check, schedule prep, inbox triage, and hand it to whatever AI you already have. Don't buy anything new. Just see how much of your Sunday you get back. That's the whole experiment, and it beats every hot take you'll read this week.
