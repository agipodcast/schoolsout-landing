01 · WHAT I BUILT

The night we gave AI agents a room

My AI helpers could not reliably talk to one another. Their shared Slack room ran on fragile connections and heartbeat messages. So the copilot and I built an isolated local room where two agents could message each other, wake each other, and challenge each other's work. Everything below is the receipts.

Now here is the moment it actually worked, and I want you to see the exact shape of it. From my phone, through dispatch, I reached Codex. Codex answered, then turned around and brought Claude Code into the mix by name. Claude answered too. Twelve seconds, start to finish, with no Slack and no heartbeat trick keeping the line warm. THE BRANCH IT DID NOT TAKE IS THE LESSON

Then we did the thing most demos skip. We shut the whole room down on purpose. The first restart failed, and I am genuinely glad it did, because it caught the room living inside a terminal window. Close the window, lose the room. That is the kind of flaw you only find by pulling the plug yourself.

03 · THE RECOVERY TEST
RESTART 1 · FAIL A temporary terminal owned the service. It died with the window.
REDESIGN The operating system owns the service now, not a window.
RESTART 2 · PASS Full machine restart, then a second clean agent handoff.
A system is not dependable until recovery has been exercised, not just described. The failed restart earned its full-size panel.

One more thing before you go. The research behind this build mapped 95 ways agent systems fail, and we boiled it down to fifteen questions. This is the exact checklist I run before any new agent gets a seat in the room. Take it, it is yours.

05 · TAKE THIS
BEFORE YOU ADD ANOTHER AGENT, ANSWER THESE 15:
01 A bounded task with a named human owner 02 A shared meaning of success 03 Only the data each task truly needs 04 Separate trust boundaries for sensitive work 05 An identity and registry for every agent 06 Short-lived, task-sized permission 07 Visible steps and human checkpoints 08 Structured, scoped memory 09 Monitoring for behavior and meaning 10 Proof of work that cannot quietly disappear 11 Cost tracking and hard limits 12 Versioned instructions and architecture 13 Backups, rollback, and secret protection 14 Open connections between different AI systems 15 Human accountability for the final decision
