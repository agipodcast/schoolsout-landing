Your Printable Checklist

Every path through this meeting ends here, because these five questions are the meeting. Print this, fold it, bring it.

  1. Show me this working with representative synthetic or properly anonymized data. Agree on realistic test cases. Do not share identifiable student records before the appropriate reviews and agreements.
  2. What does this cost in year two, at full enrollment? Get the number in writing before you fall in love.
  3. Where does our student data live, who can see it, and what happens to it if we leave? Ask for written terms and have the appropriate privacy and technology staff review them. A fast answer is not proof of compliance.
  4. What does teacher onboarding actually require, in hours, from people who have none to spare? A tool teachers don't use is a subscription, not a solution.
  5. If we cancel, what do we keep and how do we get it out? Ask about the divorce before the wedding.

One Classroom-Ready Idea

Run this simulator with your students. Seriously. Put them in the buyer's chair for any product pitch, sneakers, apps, colleges, and have them build their own five questions. Evaluating a persuasive pitch is the literacy, AI just made the stakes obvious.

One Thing To Stop Worrying About

Stop worrying that you need to understand the technology before you can question it. You question bus contracts without being a mechanic. Same skill, same you, new vendor at the door.
